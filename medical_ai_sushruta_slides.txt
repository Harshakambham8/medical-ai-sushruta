SLIDE 2: ABSTRACT & PROBLEM STATEMENT (PS)
================================================================================

[PROBLEM STATEMENT]
1. Critical Care Bottlenecks & Delayed Triage:
   - Emergency rooms and primary care clinics are routinely overwhelmed by non-urgent visits, while patients with emergent 'red-flag' conditions (cardiac events, acute stroke signs, sepsis) frequently delay clinical visits due to a lack of immediate triage understanding.
2. Medical Report Illiteracy:
   - Modern pathology reports (e.g., Complete Blood Count, Comprehensive Metabolic Panel, Lipid Profiles) present complex biochemical metrics and numerical reference ranges. Patients experience severe anxiety or overlook abnormal biomarkers due to opaque medical jargon.
3. Healthcare Ecosystem Fragmentation:
   - Current digital health tools are disjointed: symptom checkers do not analyze lab reports, report parsers do not offer triage, and neither seamlessly book qualified doctors with synchronized medical history.
4. Hallucination Risk in Generic LLMs:
   - General-purpose chatbots lack clinical guardrails, red-flag escalation protocols, and clinical safety grounding, making them unreliable for medical decision support.

[ABSTRACT]
Medical AI Sushruta is an end-to-end, high-fidelity clinical AI ecosystem built to bridge the gap between patient symptoms, diagnostic data, and physician intervention. Combining Google Gemini 2.5 Flash and Groq Ultra-Fast LPU Inference (Qwen 27B / GPT-OSS) with a resilient heuristic clinical fallback engine, Sushruta conducts multi-symptom differential risk evaluations, extracts biomarkers directly from uploaded pathology PDFs, stratifies clinical urgency (Emergency, Urgent, Moderate, Mild), and directly enables instant appointment scheduling with certified medical specialists. Designed with a cyber-glassmorphic responsive interface, an embedded zero-latency SQLite architecture, and strict Role-Based Access Control (RBAC), Sushruta provides an educational, explainable, and life-saving bridge to professional medical consultation.

[KEY HIGHLIGHT STATS]
- Up to 80% of patients research symptoms online prior to doctor visits ("Cyberchondria").
- Over 60% of lab report recipients misunderstand clinical reference ranges.
- Average appointment booking friction: 3-5 days; Sushruta connects symptoms to specialist slots in under 60 seconds.

[SPEAKER NOTES]
"The problem we are addressing is tri-fold: First, delayed patient triage leads to preventable complications. Second, patients are handed complex diagnostic lab reports without plain-language explanations, sparking confusion and anxiety. Third, existing solutions are completely fragmented—a patient uses one app for symptoms, another to store PDFs, and has to call clinics manually to find a specialist. Medical AI Sushruta provides an unified solution: intelligent triage, automated report comprehension, and immediate doctor scheduling in one single ecosystem."

SLIDE 3: OBJECTIVES
================================================================================

[PRIMARY OBJECTIVES]
1. Intelligent Symptom Differential Evaluation:
   - Implement multi-attribute symptom analysis (symptom name, severity levels, and duration timeline) to calculate clinical triage levels and identify potential disease differentials with underlying pathophysiology.

2. Automated Pathology & Biomarker Ingestion:
   - Develop an automated document extraction pipeline using `pdf-parse` and LLM reasoning to ingest lab reports, identify out-of-range biomarkers, explain physiological implications, and provide tailored physician discussion points.

3. Red-Flag Emergency Detection & Clinical Safety Guardrails:
   - Engineer deterministic safety filters and emergency prompts to instantly flag critical life-threatening conditions (e.g., crushing chest pain, anaphylaxis, severe dyspnea) with immediate first-aid directives and emergency dispatch prompts.

4. Seamless Tele-Scheduling & Doctor Discovery:

5. Longitudinal Patient Health Records HUD:

6. Multi-Role Governance & Administrative Integrity:
   - Deliver Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) supporting Patients, Verified Doctors, and Administrators with system audit trails, doctor credential verification, and financial transaction tracking.

SLIDE 4: METHODOLOGY & ARCHITECTURE
================================================================================

[SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE WORKFLOW - 5-PHASE PIPELINE]

Phase 1: Input & Multimodal Ingestion Layer
  ├── Conversational Chat Interface (Real-time clinical symptom Q&A)
  ├── Structured Symptom Matrix (Symptom name, Severity: Mild/Mod/Severe, Effective duration)
  └── Pathology Document Upload (PDF / Image Lab Reports via Multer multipart storage)

Phase 2: Pre-Processing & Text Extraction Layer
  ├── Text normalization & sanitization
  ├── Buffer parsing of uploaded medical lab PDFs via `pdf-parse`
  └── Identification of clinical biomarker tokens (e.g., HbA1c, WBC, Platelets, Creatinine, TSH)

Phase 3: Multi-Tier Hybrid Cognitive AI Reasoning Engine
  ├── Tier 1: Gemini 2.5 Flash Engine (Google Generative AI)
  │     - In-depth physiological explanations, lifestyle guidance, clinical context synthesis
  ├── Tier 2: Groq Ultra-Fast LPU Inference (`qwen/qwen3.8-27b` / `gpt-oss-120b`)
  │     - Structured differential disease matching, ICD category assignment, risk scoring (<800ms)
  └── Tier 3: Local Heuristic Rule-Based Fallback
        - Offline keyword matching, baseline triage matrix, zero-downtime high availability

Phase 4: Risk Stratification & Clinical Triage Matrix
  ├── Severity & Urgency Categorization:
  │     - CRITICAL_EMERGENCY (Immediate ER / Ambulance escalation)
  │     - URGENT_CARE (Urgent Physician visit within 12-24 hours)
  │     - MODERATE_PRIMARY_CARE (Scheduled outpatient consultation)
  │     - MILD_OBSERVATION (Self-care, hydration, symptom monitoring)
  └── Specialist Matching Recommendation (Direct mapping to appropriate medical discipline)

Phase 5: Actionable Resolution & Care Delivery
  ├── Instant layman-friendly report summary & critical biomarker alert cards
  ├── Doctor appointment booking with auto-populated symptoms and attached lab records
  └── SQLite transaction persistence & administrative audit logging

[SPEAKER NOTES]
"Our methodology is built on a 5-phase hybrid pipeline. When a user presents a symptom or uploads a lab report, the data is ingested and parsed. We employ a multi-tier AI strategy: Google Gemini 2.5 Flash handles deep clinical reasoning, Groq's high-speed LPU models handle instantaneous differential JSON structuring, and our local rule engine provides an offline zero-downtime safety net. The AI stratifies the risk into four triage categories and instantly routes the user to the appropriate medical specialist with pre-populated notes."
